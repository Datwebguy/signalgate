# Hackathon

## Event

Telegraph Hackathon Season I — Hackathon 1  
Official site: https://hackathon.telegraphprotocol.com/  
Rules: https://hackathon.telegraphprotocol.com/rules  

## Track

Track 3 — Applications & Agents  
Build real products on live Telegraph miners.

## Deadline

Submissions close: 7 September 2026, 23:59 UTC.

Winner selection: 8–18 September 2026.  
Announcement and prizes: 19–25 September 2026.

## Track 3 rules that bind this repo

- Applications must use real Telegraph miners.
- Simulated or mocked data is not allowed.
- Miners used for judging must remain live during Track 3.
- Updates used for judging must be posted publicly on X and tagged.
- Artificial inflation of metrics is disqualification.
- Join the official Hackathon Discord and stay reachable.

## What judges score for Track 3

- Users acquired and activity
- Usage and adoption
- Creativity and usefulness
- Must use Telegraph miners
- Engagement on posts that showcase the project

## What Telegraph asked builders to explore

Use these as product constraints, not slogans:

- On-chain and blockchain intelligence pipelines
- Autonomous agents and workflows
- Multi-intent and cross-domain intelligence
- Confidence thresholds and routing behavior
- Signal quality and verification
- Real-time streaming and persistent intelligence (not one-off queries)

## How Signalgate maps to the brief

- [ ] Public URL of the working app
- [ ] At least one watchlist wallet polling live
- [ ] Receipts visible in the UI
- [ ] Explorer links resolve to real requests
- [ ] X thread with tagged progress
- [ ] Discord presence
- [ ] Short demo video or screen recording
